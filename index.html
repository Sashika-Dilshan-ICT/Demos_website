About D FASHION

COLOMBO BRED. STREETWEAR EVOLVED.

D FASHION is a unisex streetwear label based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Founded with a single mission: to create luxury-grade heavyweight essentials in minimalist monochrome and desert sand tones.

We believe great style is universal. By stripping away hyper-gendered cuts and gaudy branding, our boxy drop-shoulder patterns look equally striking on anyone who appreciates authentic street culture, durable craftsmanship, and relaxed tropical wearability.

Policies & Transparency

DELIVERY, EXCHANGES & FAQS

Everything you need to know about purchasing with D FASHION in Sri Lanka.

We deliver to all addresses across Sri Lanka through trusted tracked courier partners. Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched on the same business day.

  • Colombo 1–15: 1 to 2 business days (Express same-day courier available on request via WhatsApp).
  • Greater Colombo & Suburbs: 2 business days.
  • Outstation Districts (Kandy, Galle, Kurunegala, Jaffna, etc.): 2 to 4 business days.
  • Delivery Fee: Standard Rs. 350 flat rate. Orders above Rs. 8,000 enjoy 100% FREE delivery!

We offer two straightforward, hassle-free payment methods:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay in cash directly to the courier rider when the package is handed to your door anywhere in Sri Lanka.
  • Bank Deposit / Online Bank Transfer: Transfer to our Commercial Bank or HNB corporate account and send the transfer slip to our WhatsApp line for instant dispatch confirmation.

We offer a 7-Day Hassle-Free Size Exchange Policy. If your oversized tee or cargo pants don't fit the exact way you prefer, simply message our WhatsApp support desk within 7 days of receiving your package.

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with the original barcode tag and packaging. Courier exchange riders can drop off your new size and collect the previous item simultaneously!

WhatsApp Click-to-Order allows you to purchase without creating passwords or entering sensitive credit card details into unverified forms. It gives you a direct line to our Colombo team for sizing advice, real-time parcel tracking links, and personalized customer care.

At D FASHION, we respect your privacy. Any customer details (name, phone number, delivery address) submitted through this website are used strictly for order fulfillment, courier dispatch, and customer service. We never sell or share your contact info with third-party advertisers.
